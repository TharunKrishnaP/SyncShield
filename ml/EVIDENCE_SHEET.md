## D — Incident text classifier (trained, live in backend)

- **Model**: TF-IDF (1–2 grams, sublinear) → LinearSVC, Platt-scaled
  (`CalibratedClassifierCV`) · 11 classes · trained with `ml/text/train_svc.py`
  in seconds on a laptop CPU.
- **Corpus** (`ml/data/text_corpus.jsonl`): 11,582 rows — 11,523 train
  (10,900+ template rows EN+Hinglish, 123 hand-written curated) + 59 gold
  test rows (hand-written realistic reports, **never seen by training**).

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Gold accuracy (unseen hand-written reports) | **0.7797** |
| Gold macro F1 | **0.7862** |
| Gold weighted F1 | **0.7834** |
| Template holdout accuracy | 0.9965 (weak supervision matches its own templates — gold set is the honest number) |

Per-class F1 (gold): BRIDGE_COLLAPSE 1.00 · LANDSLIDE 0.91 · TRAPPED_RESIDENTS
0.91 · RELIEF_SHELTER_FULL 0.89 · WATER_CONTAMINATION 0.89 · EVACUATION_NEEDED
0.83 · POWER_OUTAGE 0.75 · OTHER 0.77 · COMMUNICATION_DOWN 0.67 ·
FLOODED_ROAD 0.53 · HOSPITAL_ACCESS_BLOCKED 0.50.

## C — Sentinel-1 SAR flood U-Net (pipeline complete; full-IoU training on Colab)

- **Data**: Sen1Floods11 (cc-by-4.0) via HF mirror `harshinde/sen1floods`
  (~35 GB tar; S1Hand VV/VH + water labels).
- **Model**: U-Net, encoder resnet18 (imagenet-pretrained), in=2, out=1,
  BCE-with-logits + Adam, LR 1e-3.
- **Training**: `ml/sar/train_colab.ipynb` on a free T4
  (`train_unet.py --mode sen1floods11`) → val IoU/Dice reported in
  `ml/artifacts/sar_unet/meta.json`.
- **Pipeline certification on any machine**: `python ml/sar/train_unet.py
  --mode synthetic` trains a tiny net on synthetic SAR-like tiles and exercises
  train → save → load → infer → polygonise → zone attribution end-to-end
  (CI-safe; *not* a production model).
- **Integration**: `POST /api/ai/sar/ingest` (scene upload) → extent records
  (zone_id, flood_area_km2, water_depth_avg, flood_status, polygons,
  data_source "ML_SAR_UNET") → datalake → orchestrator feeds the 0.35
  satellite weight in real-time mode. Fail-soft: 501 + training pointer until
  a model exists.
